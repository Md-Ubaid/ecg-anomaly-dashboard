Label-free anomaly detection

Current ECG workflows rely on slow manual screening and supervised AI that needs labelled examples, so rare cardiac anomalies are missed.

CardioAI reconstructs each incoming window and reports its reconstruction residual. A residual indicates that the model did not reproduce the input well; it is not a diagnosis and must be reviewed with the source ECG and clinical context.
